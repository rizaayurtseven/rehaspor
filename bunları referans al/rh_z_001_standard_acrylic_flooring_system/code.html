CODE: RH-Z-001

Standard Acrylic Flooring System

A high-performance, multi-layered acrylic surface designed for professional outdoor athletic courts. Engineered for durability, UV resistance, and optimal ball bounce consistency.

Thickness 2.5 - 3.0 mm
Layers 5 Layers
UV Stability Class A
Warranty 5 Years

Technical Specifications

  • Material Base 100% Pure Acrylic Resin
  • Abrasion Resistance Taber Test < 0.1g
  • Elongation 150%
  • Hardness (Shore A) 85 - 90
  • Curing Time 24-48 Hours

Application Steps

01
Surface Preparation

Grinding and acid washing of the concrete or asphalt base.

02
Acrylic Primer

Application of epoxy-based primer for maximum adhesion.

03
Resurfacer Layer

Filling of minor pores and creating a level base for color.

04
Color Coats

Two layers of UV-stable acrylic pigmented coating.

Built for Industrial Durability

Our proprietary acrylic resin blend is formulated to withstand extreme temperature fluctuations (-30°C to +50°C) without cracking or delamination. The RH-Z-001 system utilizes micro-silica sand integration for uniform texture and safety.
